Data availability

The Indonesian Government Applications Review (IGAR) dataset analyzed in this study is publicly available through Mendeley Data, Version 3, DOI: 10.17632/7zryc6k76z.3. The analyses used the dataset-provided review text, rating-derived sentiment labels, translation field, and VADER-derived sentiment variables as released. The dataset-provided translation and VADER outputs were not regenerated for the present study.
